Privacy Policy

Effective: August 11, 2026

DevDisk does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, account, or payment system.

Filesystem data

File names, paths, project information, scan results, and detected developer artifacts are processed locally on your Mac and are not uploaded by DevDisk. Filesystem access is limited to locations you select through the macOS file picker and any access allowed by macOS.

Local storage

DevDisk stores the latest scan, aggregated scan history, application settings, and security-scoped bookmarks in its local app container. This information remains on your Mac.

CSV exports

CSV reports are created only when you request an export and are written to the location you choose. Reports may contain local paths and project names; review them before sharing.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the DevDisk support tracker. Do not include private paths or reports in a public issue.
